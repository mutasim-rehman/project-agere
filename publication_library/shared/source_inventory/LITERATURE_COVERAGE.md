# Literature coverage decisions

This is a comparison of the old bibliography's scientific role, not a certification of every excluded old bibliographic claim. Retained references have primary-source provenance in the revised `citation_coverage.json`. Excluded external performance numbers and unverified venue claims are not repeated as evidence. Original entries remain unchanged in the 37-page manuscript.
