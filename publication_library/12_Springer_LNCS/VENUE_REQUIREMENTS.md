# Springer LNCS/LNAI proceedings requirements

Verified2026-11-12. Template/instructions: https://link.springer.com/series/558/information-for-authors-and-editors

Official publisher-linked LNCS class/BST; provenance in template/provenance.json.

Policy source: https://www.springernature.com/gp/policies/book-publishing-policies

AI assistance requires transparent disclosure and human accountability. Final verification remains with authors. Event-specific page caps, review anonymity, supplement acceptance and registration/fees must be verified after selecting a conference. Publisher permits electronic supplementary material; a particular event may impose tighter rules.

Actual authors must confirm correctness/originality, identities, disclosures, conflicts, licenses, anonymous evidence release and final portal requirements. Do not submit concurrent archival alternatives.
