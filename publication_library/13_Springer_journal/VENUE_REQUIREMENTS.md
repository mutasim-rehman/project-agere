# Springer Nature generic journal template

Verified 2026-11-12: https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/campaigns/latex-author-support
Official December 2024 kit, version 3.1, downloaded from the publisher-linked URL; hash/file inventory in shared/official_templates/springer.provenance.json. Uses sn-jnl with sn-mathphys-num, its own bibliography style. Main source is one flattened TeX file, as instructed. Specific journals may override these defaults. This generic version is not yet tied to a journal scope, fee, deadline or submission-ready determination.

AI policy: https://www.springernature.com/gp/policies/editorial-policies/ai-manuscript-preparation . Human accountability and disclosure review required. Numerical graphics derive from independently verified data. Author identities/funding/conflicts remain for human completion. No novel theorem or publication eligibility is asserted.
