# Submission roadmap — one contribution, alternative formats

This is an editorial fit assessment, not an estimate of acceptance probability. No submission is authorized or performed. Select one archival destination at a time; do not present these versions as independent papers.

## Highly ambitious

AAAI, ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML and IJCAI are ambitious because the study is model/prompt/task-specific, recipes confound scale/precision/pipeline, sparse prospective gains remain statistically uncertain and some baseline/resource evidence is incomplete. The contribution is empirical/critical failure analysis, not a new winning algorithm. NeurIPS has a relevant negative-results contribution framing; this does not lower the scientific bar. AAAI/IJCAI require independently human-written prose under their verified policies. ICLR2027 has a different disclosed-AI-use policy; the2026 workshops checked here prohibit generated papers. Closed editions/templates require future refresh, not a claim of current availability.

## Suggested sequence

Actual authors first review the corrected current drafts, evidence limits, authorship and disclosures. Then choose an archival destination based on scope, desired length, budget and policy compliance. Prepare a single compliant submission and its evidence package. A preprint is an option only after checking the target's current anonymity/overlap rules, arXiv account/category/endorsement/license obligations and author approval. After a decision/withdrawal, adapt to the next destination if needed. Do not manufacture new experiments or strengthen conclusions to fit a venue.
