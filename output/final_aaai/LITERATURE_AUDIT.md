# Primary-source literature audit

Verified 11 November 2026. `citation_audit.json` records primary metadata provenance; `references.bib` contains complete author lists from those sources. arXiv entries are cited as accessible research versions, without invented proceedings details. Published metadata are taken directly from ACL/PMLR. Prior working-note citations were not automatically inherited.

- **FinQA (Chen et al., 2021)** — [ACL paper](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.300/). Establishes the financial report/program benchmark. We use subsets and disclose local versus official scoring. No comparison to its leaderboard numbers is implied.
- **TAT-QA (Zhu et al., 2021)** — [ACL paper](https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.254/). Establishes hybrid table/text QA and the benchmark task types. Our arithmetic-only cohorts are not full-benchmark evaluations.
- **PAL (Gao et al., 2023)** — [PMLR paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/gao23f.html). Interpreter execution removes arithmetic burden while program construction remains a model task. We use a restricted source-referenced grammar rather than replicate PAL's prompts, models or Python execution setup.
- **Program of Thoughts (Chen et al., 2023)** — [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.12588v4). Explicitly includes financial datasets and semantic binding; prevents claims that executable financial reasoning or separating computation is new. Our findings qualify a specific small-model implementation, not the published result.
- **Multiagent Debate (Du et al., 2024)** — [PMLR paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/du24e.html). Positive evidence for iterative debate in other systems. Our specialist pipeline is not a debate replication, and hardware/token budgets differ.
- **Self-Consistency (Wang et al., 2023)** — [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171). Multiple sampled reasoning paths and marginalization are established. Our greedy program diagnostics do not test this method or support claims against it.
- **Equal Thinking Token Budgets (Tran and Kiela, 2026)** — [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02460v2). Close prior for controlled SAS/MAS evaluation. Our application memory ceilings and generated-token ceilings are not equivalent to exact thinking-token or FLOP matching. Our deployment recipes also differ in capacity and precision.
- **MAST (Cemri et al., 2025)** — [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657). Broader multiagent failure taxonomy. We do not claim a first general taxonomy or transfer its failure percentages to our system.
- **Self-Refine (Madaan et al., 2023)** — [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651). Positive prior for self-feedback/refinement. Our negative repair outcomes are conditional on our generators, prompts and tasks.
- **Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet (Huang et al., 2024)** — [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798). Negative evidence for intrinsic correction without external feedback. Our deterministic execution supplies limited external checks; the two settings are not identical.
- **CRITIC (Gou et al., 2024)** — [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11738). Tool-interactive feedback and correction are established; prevents novelty claims for executor feedback alone.
- **VeriFin (Hall et al., 2026)** — [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10213v1). Close financial verification preprint: XBRL-grounded operands and authorized formulas with SMT. Our source membership is weaker, and no matched replication was run. Its zero-false-accept observations are not a guarantee for either method.
- **BudgetVerify (Jenq and Shen, 2026)** — [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33052v1). Close financial QA preprint using learned allocation among verification tiers. We do not claim originality for routing or cost-aware verification, and do not import its accuracy-cost frontier.
- **Qwen2.5 (Qwen et al., 2024)** — [technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15115). Model-family provenance; exact checkpoint files and hashes come from our manifests. The distinct Qwen2.5-1M report was screened out as the wrong family variant.
- **LoRA (Hu et al., 2021)** — [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685). Low-rank adaptation is established; role adapters are baselines, not an invented contribution.
- **QLoRA (Dettmers et al., 2023)** — [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314). Quantized-base adaptation and NF4 context. Our actual training recipe and GPU memory are separately recorded; the published large-model memory claims are not applied to Agere.
- **SelectiveNet (Geifman and El-Yaniv, 2019)** — [PMLR paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/geifman19a.html). Coverage/error tradeoff and reject options are established. Our binary gate has no learned calibration or certified risk guarantee.

Search scope covered financial program reasoning, multiagent budget controls and failures, correction/verification, adaptation, and selective prediction. This is a focused verified review, not an exhaustive systematic review. No experimental replication of external methods is claimed. Full benchmark comparisons or novelty beyond the bounded empirical sequence would require additional evidence; none is invented here.
