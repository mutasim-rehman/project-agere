# Claim-to-evidence map

All paths below are repository-relative unless prefixed `preserved/` (restored by `dashboard_handoff/restore_evidence.py`). No original outcome file is overwritten by the paper scripts.

- **Six Ubuntu jobs, paired 160 questions:** preserved `runs/lab_tiers_v1/lab-16-ubuntu-noswap-v1/tier16-*/cases.jsonl`; reconciled to `dashboard_handoff/data/tier16_ubuntu/case_outcomes.json`. New results: `verified_tier16.json`. Initial and final execution/scoring: `raw_score_verification.json`.
- **No sampled process swap; peak RSS; interruptions:** preserved per-attempt memory, state and scope logs; `dashboard_handoff/data/tier16_ubuntu/resource_events.json`, `preservation.json`. Audit scans every retained memory sample. Does not validate unsampled peaks or uninterrupted operation.
- **16 GB candidate shortage and metric discordance:** raw initial/final programs plus `research/tier16_v2/FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md`, `data/metric_disagreements.csv`. Score definition checked against `scripts/tier8_v2_financial_pipeline.py`; tolerance is absolute/relative, not absolute-only.
- **Planner criterion and design:** `research/tier8_v2/WINDOWS_PLANNER_STUDY.md`, frozen cohort files and raw ledgers under `evidence/windows-planner-study/`. Independent official re-score and full outcome transitions: `verified_planner.json`.
- **Sparse positive bootstrap, exact p=.125, wrong increase:** raw paired predictions, archived `tatqa-dev-scores.json`, reproduced in `verified_planner.json`. Both inferential views retained; no reliable or practical win claimed.
- **Shape-use and tree agreement:** `tatqa-dev-scores.json` and raw shape predictions, plus `scripts/tier8_windows_planner_study.py`. Tree equality is not full algebraic equivalence.
- **Incomplete secondary 7B comparison:** `tatqa-test-large-initial-prefix.jsonl`, `tatqa-test-large-recovery-partial.jsonl`, and `progress.json`. Two cases do not become 28-case scores.
- **Q4/Q8, catalog, oracle matrix, capacity, format, transfer:** `research/tier8_v2/evidence/windows-extended/`, plus the corresponding pilot directories. Fourteen paired FinQA ledgers are re-executed/rescored; TAT-QA prediction ledgers re-scored. Raw checks and input hashes accompany the paper.
- **Evidence witnesses and endpoint diagnostics:** `windows-extended/finqa96-endpoint-structure.json`, capacity/coverage audit files, `WINDOWS_EXTENDED_RESEARCH.md`, `WINDOWS_ENDPOINT_STUDY.md`. Oracle potential remains explicitly answer-key dependent.
- **Retrieval failure to replicate:** `windows-retrieval-study/inference-summary.json`, `replication-summary.json`, raw ledgers and development coverage audits. A proposed fixed-catalog follow-up is not reported as completed.
- **Verification failures and favorable constructed probes:** Windows verifier/reject/natural/adapter/critic directories; earlier Linux `CRITIC_ARTIFACT_AUDIT.md`, `MATCHED_MAS_SELECTION.md`, `research/lab_tiers/EIGHT_GB_CLOSURE.json`. Full historical condition census printed in supplement.
- **Training, failed fits, exact adapters:** archived plans/manifests; `reproducibility/identities.json`; initial/continuation completion evidence. Token loss and application proof are never substituted for QA accuracy.
- **Complete record retention:** `artifact_inventory.json` hashes 3,776 files; `all_result_summaries.json` retains 222 selected summary/result/score/analysis documents without outcome filtering. `DECISIONS.md` remains untouched. This inventory is not a claim of independent semantic adjudication of every historical artifact.

Known gaps are stated in the main limitations and supplement: Windows physical 8 GB/paging, TAT-QA report clusters, incomplete secondary 7B, independent semantic annotation review, multi-seed/model-family uncertainty, missing original Ubuntu export/log, unmeasured 32 GB tier.
