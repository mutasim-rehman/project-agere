# Submission checks and truthful disclosure

## Completed scientific and document checks

- Raw scoring/execution and archived summary reconciliation are recorded in the audit JSONs; historical outcomes are unchanged.
- Main claims distinguish recipe comparisons, development results, oracle diagnostics and project-held-out evaluation.
- Correct/wrong/withheld denominators, paired transitions, report/context clustering, sparse-discordance uncertainty, shared cohorts and missing baselines are disclosed.
- Model identities, adaptation, precision, CPU/runtime settings, token/resource ceilings, evaluation hashes and prompt availability are documented.
- Seventeen real primary references are verified; no bibliography was copied blindly from prior working notes.
- Main paper and separate supplement compile with PDFLaTeX/BibTeX. `compilation_checks.json` records page sizes/counts, unresolved-reference and overflow checks. `visual_review.json` records the final visual review.
- No new experiments, labels, model outputs, confidence claims or author identities were fabricated.

## Required before actual submission

1. Authenticate the style/BibTeX files against the official author kit; its download currently returns HTTP 403. Mirror provenance is documented, not represented as official-byte verification.
2. Confirm the actual venue/year, deadline, supplement rules and current official reproducibility checklist. This document maps the available evidence but is not a signed or completed copy of an inaccessible official form.
3. Confirm author list, affiliations, contributions, conflicts, funding and any required publication declarations. None can be inferred from experimental files.
4. Inspect the evidence/source package for double-blind compliance before uploading it. Original paths and project provenance identify the research environment; the main manuscript itself is anonymous.
5. Authors must review the scientific text and accept responsibility for it. Manuscript drafting, code-assisted checking and plotting used an AI assistant; no new model inference for the research was performed during this preparation. Apply the venue's current AI-use disclosure requirements truthfully.

These administrative checks do not imply that new experiments are necessary. The acknowledged scientific limits are part of the paper, not missing numbers to fill with speculation.
