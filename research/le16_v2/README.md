`data/cross_tier_analysis.json` preserves the separate8 GB synthetic,8 GB adapted FinQA and 16 GB pretrained FinQA regimes. `tier8_condition_inventory.csv` contains15 completed numerical conditions; `tier8_case_outcomes.csv` has 768 repeated outputs on 101 unique questions with selection/reserved cohort labels for independent re-analysis. `tier8_evidence_catalog.json` indexes197 historical artifacts without treating them as independent experiments. No pooled causal RAM effect,32 GB extrapolation or algorithmic-novelty proof is claimed.
