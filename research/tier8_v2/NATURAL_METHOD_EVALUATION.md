architecture, with the same initializer, optimizer recipe, data exposure and
inference policy. MAS uses the same common continued actor for both conditions;
SAS adapts its shared policy, so its drafting can also change. Report whole-policy

architectures on the reserved reports even if only one looks promising in selection.

fixed training examples for baseline/control/candidate alike; actual fitting may
use fewer demos. If time prevents these, disclose incomplete prompt optimality.

Common primary policy is the existing reference program, compact catalog,
question-unit grammar, direct drafting without analysis/demos, binary review,
and one full regeneration after a rejection. Stage limits768/192/768/192 fit the
2048 generated-token ledger. Secondary analysis128 fits that same ledger at the

Replaying initial draft/review prefixes yields no-repair results and measured
prefix costs; ungated executable initial drafts are reported separately. Do not
assign final-trajectory work to the prefix or call it fresh counterfactual generation.

Primary outcomes: correct delivered, wrong delivered and withheld under BOTH
reported-unit and raw-execution numerical scoring, with actual prefill/decode,
time and complete resident RSS. Promising means no decrease in correct delivery
and no increase in wrong delivery under both metrics, with at least one strict
gain. Tradeoffs/conflicting metrics are not an unqualified improvement. Report
whole-report cluster intervals; no outcome-selected scalar penalty. Small reserved
samples cannot certify financial safety. Synthetic quartet accuracy or lower
teacher-forced loss alone cannot justify adoption or a novelty claim.
